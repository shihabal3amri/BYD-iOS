1. Set up AltStore Classic

Already have AltStore Classic? Continue to the next step. Otherwise, install AltServer on your computer and use it to install AltStore Classic on your iPhone. Use your own Apple account; no paid developer membership is required. Enable Developer Mode on iOS 16 or later.

The videos explain AltStore setup. Screens and steps may differ by version; use the current official guides if needed: macOS guide · Windows guide.

2. Install BYD or update

  1. Tap Add to AltStore Classic above and confirm adding the source.
  2. Find BYD Localized in AltStore, review permissions and install with your own Apple account.
  3. Open BYD and sign in to your own BYD account. Go to Me → Settings to choose your language and bottom tabs.

Already installed? Refresh the source in AltStore, then choose Update for BYD under My Apps. Look for 9.16.0 · Revision 2. Keep your existing app and use the same Apple account to preserve its local data.

Keep AltServer running and reachable over the same Wi-Fi network or USB when installing, updating or refreshing. With a free Apple account, refresh both AltStore and BYD before their seven-day signing period expires. The download is about 339 MB; speed depends on your connection to GitHub. Read the full guide.

Installing with Impactor

Download the IPA and import it into Impactor. On the package options screen, change Name from the Chinese text to BYD, then sign and install with your own Apple account.

If you see Developer API error 35 about a missing appIdName, set Name to BYD and retry with the same IPA. Keep the default identifier and app-group handling. Downloading an IPA alone does not install it. Read the Impactor instructions.

Make it yours

  • Choose English, Arabic or Russian in Settings.
  • Hide Discover, Mall and Service individually.
  • Find My Car by its original car icon.

Fully close and reopen BYD after changing language. Tab visibility changes immediately; My Car and Me remain available.

Public beta · Revision 2

Revision 2 restores Bluetooth badges on supported dashboard controls. It has installed and launched through AltStore Classic on a non-jailbroken iPhone XS. The maintainer also reports successful testing on several other iPhones; broader feedback is welcome.

Requires iOS 15 or later; not every device or vehicle function has been tested. Some Chinese content and artwork remain. The profile page may be blank. Wakeup unlock is not included yet.
